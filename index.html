Main Title for Document: The Trombone

I have been playing the trombone since the 4th grade. The trombone is a cool instrument. The trombone has a slide, a mouthpiece, and a body that includes the bell at the end. It is a brass instrument that has its music written in bass clef.

An Aside: Playing the trombone was a big part of my life, so I enjoy sharing stuff like this.
